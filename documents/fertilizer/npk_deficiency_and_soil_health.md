# Soil Fertility and Nutrient Deficiency Diagnostics
## Major Nutrients
### Nitrogen (N)
- **Deficiency Symptoms**: General chlorosis (yellowing) starting from older lower leaves progressing to upper leaves; stunted plant vigor.
- **Correction**: Split application of Urea or enriched Farm Yard Manure (FYM).

### Phosphorus (P)
- **Deficiency Symptoms**: Purplish discoloration on leaves and stems, poor root architecture, delayed flowering.
- **Correction**: Single Super Phosphate (SSP) or Rock Phosphate with PSB (Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria).

### Potassium (K)
- **Deficiency Symptoms**: Marginal scorching, leaf tip burn, weak stems prone to lodging.
- **Correction**: Muriate of Potash (MOP) or Sulfate of Potash (SOP).
