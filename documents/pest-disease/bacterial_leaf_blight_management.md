# Comprehensive Bacterial Leaf Blight Management Protocol
## Identification Matrix
- **Differential Diagnosis**: Unlike fungal blast spots which have distinct spindle shapes, bacterial leaf blight lesions have undulating margins along leaf veins.
- **Rapid Field Test**: Cut an infected leaf section and place in clean glass of water; milky bacterial streaming confirms bacterial blight.

## Action Plan
- Stage 1 (Initial detection < 5% severity): Withhold nitrogen, spray Cow Dung Filtrate (5%) or Pseudomonas fluorescens @ 10 g/L.
- Stage 2 (5-20% severity): Copper Oxychloride 50 WP @ 2.5 g/L + Agrimycin/Streptocycline 1 g/10 L.
- Stage 3 (>20% severe): Prevent irrigation water movement from infected fields to healthy fields to curtail bacterial spread.
