# Fall Armyworm (Spodoptera frugiperda) and Stem Borer Protocol
## Affected Crops: Maize, Sorghum, Millets, Rice
### Symptoms
- Pinholes and window-pane feeding on whorl leaves.
- Deep ragged holes and copious sawdust-like frass in leaf whorls.

### Management Strategy
- Pheromone traps @ 5 per acre for monitoring.
- Release of egg parasitoid *Trichogramma chilonis* @ 50,000/acre.
- Sand + Lime or Wood Ash application directly into whorls (ITK method).
- Spray *Bacillus thuringiensis* (Bt) @ 2 g/liter or Emamectin benzoate 5% SG @ 0.4 g/liter for severe infestations.
