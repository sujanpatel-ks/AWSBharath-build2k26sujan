# Rice (Paddy) Cultivation, Pest and Disease Management
## Overview
Rice (Oryza sativa) is a primary staple crop in India, extensively cultivated across Karnataka, Punjab, Andhra Pradesh, and West Bengal.

## Key Diseases and Symptoms
### 1. Bacterial Leaf Blight (Xanthomonas oryzae pv. oryzae)
- **Symptoms**: Water-soaked stripes on leaf margins turning yellow to straw-colored; bacterial ooze droplets visible in early morning.
- **Predisposing Factors**: Warm temperatures (25-34°C), high relative humidity (>70%), excessive nitrogen fertilization.
- **Management Protocol**:
  - Balanced fertilization: Apply N in split doses; avoid excess urea.
  - ITK / Organic remedy: Spray Fresh Cow Dung Filtrate (5%) or Neem Cake Extract (5%).
  - Chemical control (if severe): Streptocycline 100 ppm + Copper Oxychloride 0.25%.

### 2. Blast Disease (Magnaporthe oryzae)
- **Symptoms**: Diamond or spindle-shaped lesions with grayish centers and brown borders on leaves (Leaf Blast) and blackened neck nodes (Neck Blast).
- **Organic protocol**: Tricoderma viride @ 5g/liter foliar spray.
- **Chemical protocol**: Tricyclazole 75% WP @ 0.6 g/liter water.

## Spray Window Guidelines
- Do not apply foliar sprays if rain is forecasted within 4 hours.
- Ideal spray timing: Early morning (7-10 AM) or late afternoon (4-6 PM) when wind velocity is < 15 km/h.
