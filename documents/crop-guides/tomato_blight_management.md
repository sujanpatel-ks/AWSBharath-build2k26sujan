# Tomato Blight and Pest Management Guide
## Overview
Tomato (Solanum lycopersicum) is susceptible to both Early Blight and Late Blight under humid conditions.

## Disease Profiles
### 1. Early Blight (Alternaria solani)
- **Symptoms**: Concentric ring spot lesions ('target board' appearance) on older lower leaves, advancing upwards.
- **Treatment**: Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/liter or Copper Hydroxide @ 2 g/liter.
- **ITK Practice**: Buttermilk spray (sour buttermilk diluted 1:10 with water) effective in early fungal suppression.

### 2. Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans)
- **Symptoms**: Dark water-soaked lesions on leaves and stems with white fungal growth on undersides during high humidity; rapid vine collapse.
- **Treatment**: Cymoxanil 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2 g/liter.
- **Safety Window**: Rain-washout risk high. Minimum 6 hours dry interval needed.
