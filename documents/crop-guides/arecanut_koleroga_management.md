# Arecanut Fruit Rot (Koleroga / Mahali) Guide
## Overview
Arecanut (Areca catechu) in the Western Ghats and coastal Karnataka faces severe crop loss due to Koleroga caused by *Phytophthora meadii*.

## Symptoms and Diagnosis
- Water-soaked dark green lesions on the base of developing arecanut fruitlets.
- Premature shedding of button nuts and heavy fruit drop.
- Foul-smelling rotting nuts on the orchard floor with visible white mycelial growth.

## Integrated Treatment & ITK
1. **Sanitation**: Collect and burn all dropped and infected nuts to eliminate inoculum.
2. **Prophylactic Spray**: 1% Bordeaux Mixture (Copper sulphate 1 kg + Lime 1 kg in 100 L water) applied before the onset of monsoon.
3. **ITK Covering**: Polythene bag covering of nut bunches before heavy monsoon rains.
4. **Bio-control**: Trichoderma harzianum colonized neem cake applied around tree basin.
