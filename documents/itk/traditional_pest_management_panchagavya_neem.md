# Traditional Pest Management & Safety Protocol
## Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE 5%)
- **Target Pests**: Diamondback moth, Helicoverpa armigera, Whiteflies, Thrips.
- **Preparation**: 50 g powdered clean neem seed kernels soaked overnight in 1 L water, filtered, with 1 g soap powder added as surfactant.
- **Safety**: Safe for honeybees, predatory mites, and beneficial insects. 0 days withholding period.

## Safety Guidelines for Farmers
- Wear protective cloth masks and gloves during preparation and application.
- Never spray against the wind direction.
- Dispose of empty containers safely; never wash equipment in natural water bodies.
