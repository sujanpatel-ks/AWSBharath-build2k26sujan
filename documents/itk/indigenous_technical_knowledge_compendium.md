# Indigenous Technical Knowledge (ITK) in Indian Agriculture
## ICAR Validated Traditional Formulations

### 1. Panchagavya Formulation
- **Ingredients**: Cow dung (5 kg), Cow urine (3 L), Cow milk (2 L), Cow curd (2 L), Cow ghee (1 kg), Jaggery (500 g), Tender coconut water (3 L), Ripened banana (12 nos).
- **Preparation**: Ferment for 15-21 days with daily stirring.
- **Application**: 3% foliar spray (30 ml in 1 L water) as a systemic growth promoter and immunity booster against fungal pathogens.

### 2. Dashaparni Kashayam
- **Ingredients**: Leaves of 10 non-edible medicinal plants (Neem, Pongamia, Calotropis, Adhatoda, Datura, Papaya, Custard apple, Castor, Guava, Marigold) fermented in cow urine and dung for 30 days.
- **Action**: Broad-spectrum pest repellent and anti-feedant against chewing and sucking pests.
- **Dosage**: 200 ml per 10 L water.

### 3. Agniastra (Organic Pest Controller)
- **Ingredients**: 1 kg crushed tobacco leaves, 500 g hot green chili paste, 500 g garlic paste, 5 kg crushed neem leaves boiled in 15 L cow urine.
- **Action**: Controls leaf miners, borer larvae, and aphids.
